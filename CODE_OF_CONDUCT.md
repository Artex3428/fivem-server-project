# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors and community members are expected to communicate respectfully
and professionally.

Constructive disagreement about code, architecture, design, gameplay, or
project direction is welcome.

Personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption,
or abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Technical Discussion

Feedback should focus on the work rather than the person.

Examples of appropriate discussion include:

- Explaining why an implementation may be insecure
- Pointing out performance problems
- Disagreeing about architecture or design choices
- Suggesting alternative approaches
- Requesting additional testing or evidence

Strong technical disagreement is acceptable when it remains relevant and
respectful.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or targeted abuse
- Threats
- Discriminatory attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions
- Repeated spam or intentionally misleading reports
- Attempting to exploit project infrastructure or community members

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject contributions,
discussions, issues, or other participation that violates these standards.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restrictions or bans from project
spaces.
